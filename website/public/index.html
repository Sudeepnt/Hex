A few things
before you speak.

Does my speech leave my computer?

Local dictation runs on your device after the model downloads. Optional online integrations you enable can use external services.

Will this work on my Mac?

This download supports Apple silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later. An Intel Mac installer is not currently offered.

Why does Utter need Accessibility access?

On macOS, Utter uses Accessibility access to handle your dictation shortcut and paste text into the app you’re using.
